Agricultural Schemes & Subsidies

Central Government ₹6,000 / Year DBT

PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)

Direct income support of ₹2,000 every 4 months deposited directly into the farmer's bank account.

Eligibility: All small and marginal farmer families holding cultivable land in their names.
Required Documents: Aadhaar Card, Land Patta/Chitta copy, NPCI-linked Bank Passbook.
How to Apply: Register on pmkisan.gov.in or visit nearest CSC/e-Sevai center. Field verified by Block AAO.
Block ADA Office • Toll-free: 155261
Tamil Nadu + Central 100% Free Subsidy

Tamil Nadu Micro-Irrigation Scheme (PMKSY Drip & Sprinkler)

Full 100% subsidy for small/marginal farmers (up to 5 acres) and 75% for other farmers for complete drip/sprinkler installation.

Eligibility: Farmers having cultivable land with borewell, open well, or farm pond.
Required Documents: Patta, Chitta, FMB Sketch, Water & Soil test report, Aadhaar, Bank Passbook.
How to Apply: Apply via Tamil Nadu Uzhavan App or visit Block Assistant Director of Horticulture (ADH).
Block ADH Office • Helpline: 1800-425-4444
Central Government Only 1.5% Premium

PMFBY (Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana - Crop Insurance)

Comprehensive risk coverage against drought, floods, cyclone, localized hailstorm, and post-harvest losses.

Eligibility: Farmers cultivating notified crops (Paddy, Cotton, Maize, Groundnut) in notified villages.
Required Documents: Sowing Certificate from VAO, Adangal extract, Land Patta, Aadhaar.
How to Apply: Enroll at nearest Primary Agricultural Credit Society (PACCS), Commercial Bank, or CSC center before cut-off date (Nov 15 for Samba).
Local PACCS Bank / CSC Center

Required Documents: Patta/Chitta, VAO Well Certificate, Ground water clearance, Aadhaar.
How to Apply: Register on aed.tn.gov.in (Agricultural Engineering Department).
