## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an authentic synthesis of high-tier modern enterprise SaaS and grounded agricultural vitality. Engineered primarily for progressive farmers, agri-tech operators, agronomists, and supply-chain stakeholders, the interface projects reliability, biological precision, and warmth.

The aesthetic blends **Modern SaaS Functionalism** with **Organic Tactility**:
- Crisp, hyper-legible utility designed to withstand harsh outdoor sunlight and high-glare field environments.
- Warm, earthy material sensibility that honors the physical balance between rich arable soil and flourishing crops.
- Intentional support for bilingual typography workflows (English alongside native Tamil typographic rhythms), prioritizing clear glyph distinction, open tracking, and high optical contrast.
- Visual elements feel alive, structured, and dependable—eschewing clinical coldness in favor of fertile greens, loam-tinted neutrals, and harvest accents.

## Colors

The palette establishes an organic, grounded rhythm derived directly from agricultural soil and active vegetation, engineered to surpass WCAG AAA standards for sunlight visibility.

### Primary & Botanical Greens
- **Primary Forest Green (`#14532D`)**: The anchor of the system; applied to primary navigation, key action CTAs, active operational indicators, and authoritative headers.
- **Deep Agricultural Green (`#166534`)**: Mid-tier botanical tone used for interactive hover states, metric banners, and primary badge accents.
- **Fresh Leaf Green (`#4D7C0F`)**: Accent hue for growth rates, active sensor telemetry, healthy crop health statuses, and positive indicators.
- **Soft Sage Green (`#E8F2E8`)**: Tonal surface background for positive state pill tags, soft card highlights, and secondary button fills.

### Soil & Earth Tones
- **Earthy Soil Brown (`#795548`)**: Secondary interactive elements, soil nutrition indexes, and field-zone telemetry identifiers.
- **Rich Fertile Brown (`#5D4037`)**: Supporting brand elements, secondary structural buttons, categorical tabs, and geological data structures.
- **Light Soil Beige (`#F3E9D8`)**: Subdued container surface for yield tracking, soil moisture cards, and warm auxiliary banners.

### Functional Accents & Neutrals
- **Harvest Gold (`#D4A72C`)**: Used selectively for pending reviews, harvest-ready alerts, equipment caution states, and premium feature markers.
- **Warm Canvas Background (`#F8F7F0`)**: Base ambient canvas replacing stark clinical white with a low-strain, sun-adaptive organic off-white.
- **Card Surface (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure elevated surface for critical data containers, modular cards, and floating modals.
- **Main Text (`#24352A`)**: Deep botanical graphite for maximum readability and zero color fatigue.
- **Secondary Text (`#647067`)**: Calibrated slate-green for captions, helper text, and secondary metric labels.
- **Delicate Borders**: `#DDE7DD` across plant-focused modules and `#E6DFD3` across earth-focused cards.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy prioritizes rapid optical parsing, high tall-glyph clarity, and bilingual cadence support (Tamil alongside English):
- **Universal Sans Scale**: Driven by `Inter` across all structural tiers to deliver neutral, geometrically balanced letterforms with generous aperture openings.
- **Bilingual Stacking Pattern**: Secondary Tamil labels sit directly underneath primary English descriptors using `bilingual-hint` styling in `#647067`. Tamil titles match primary metric weight but scale down to 85% relative point size to maintain baseline optical balance.
- **High Glare Optimization**: Headline weights remain constrained between semi-bold (600) and bold (700). Avoid thin and ultra-light weights across all field displays.
- **Numeric Precision**: Tabular numerals are mandatory for all sensor metrics, financial yield counters, moisture ratios, and weight scales.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column grid system tuned for dense sensor telemetry and touch-first tablet and mobile operations:
- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 32px (`2rem`) outer margins. Content maximum container width capped at 1440px.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns, 20px gutters, 24px outer margins. Metric cards stack in pairs.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 16px (`1rem`) outer margins. Data tables convert to card decks with sticky bottom controls.
- **Touch-First Spatial Rule**: All interactive targets adhere to a minimum operational footprint of 48px × 48px to accommodate outdoor and gloved usage.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system departs from generic grey drop shadows, utilizing warm, organic soil-tinted ambient projections to maintain spatial grounding against the `#F8F7F0` canvas.

- **Base Level (Canvas)**: Non-elevated surface set to `#F8F7F0`.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Surface)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` fill bounded by a crisp 1px perimeter border (`#DDE7DD` or `#E6DFD3`). Shadow: `0 2px 6px -1px rgba(93, 64, 55, 0.04), 0 1px 3px -1px rgba(20, 83, 45, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Interactive Focus)**: Elevated state for clickable summary cards. Shadow: `0 10px 20px -3px rgba(93, 64, 55, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(20, 83, 45, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Overlays, Sliders, Modals)**: Critical diagnostic drawers and popovers. Shadow: `0 20px 30px -4px rgba(36, 53, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -3px rgba(93, 64, 55, 0.06)`.
- **Outline Strategy**: Soft dual-tone ghost borders define spatial depth without heavy visual weight, ensuring layouts remain calm under intensive data densities.

## Shapes

The shape architecture relies on softened, approachable organic geometry that prevents the industrial severity often seen in legacy enterprise agriculture tools:
- **Card Containers**: Standardized to `rounded-2xl` (16px / 1rem radius) to evoke contemporary SaaS polish while delivering soft visual transitions across juxtaposed data blocks.
- **Action Buttons & Form Controls**: Standardized to 10px–12px radius, balancing structural integrity with tactile softness.
- **Badges, Tags, & Status Pills**: Rendered in full pill radius (`rounded-full`) to immediately differentiate categorical metadata from interactive cards.
- **Micro Radii**: Small graphical meters, segmented indicators, and internal card compartments use 6px–8px radius.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#14532D` fill, pure `#FFFFFF` typography (`title-md`), 12px vertical padding, 20px horizontal padding, 10px border radius. Hover: `#166534`. Active: `#0f3d21`.
- **Secondary (Soil)**: Solid `#5D4037` fill, `#FFFFFF` text. Used exclusively for secondary actions (e.g., Export Soil Diagnostics, Zone Config).
- **Tertiary (Ghost Leaf)**: Background `#E8F2E8`, text `#14532D`, border 1px solid `#DDE7DD`. Hover: background shifts to `#d8ead8`.
- **Icon Sizing**: 20px inside buttons, matched strictly to label contrast color.

### Cards
- **Base Style**: `#FFFFFF` background, `rounded-2xl` (16px), 1.5px border (`#DDE7DD` for crop health, `#E6DFD3` for soil/weather metrics). Padding: 20px mobile, 24px desktop.
- **Header Structure**: Top metadata bar includes primary title, optional bilingual Tamil caption (`bilingual-hint`), and a status chip pinned to the upper right.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status (Positive/Growth)**: `#E8F2E8` background, `#14532D` text, 1px solid rgba(20, 83, 45, 0.15), `rounded-full`, 4px vertical / 12px horizontal padding.
- **Status (Soil/Neutral)**: `#F3E9D8` background, `#5D4037` text, 1px solid rgba(93, 64, 55, 0.15).
- **Status (Yield Alert/Caution)**: Background rgba(212, 167, 44, 0.15), text `#8f6e14`, 1px solid rgba(212, 167, 44, 0.3).

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Input Fields**: Surface `#FFFFFF`, border 1.5px solid `#DDE7DD`, text `#24352A`, placeholder `#647067`. Focus state: 1.5px border `#14532D` with an ambient glow ring of `rgba(20, 83, 45, 0.12)`. Height: 48px.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 20px size, 1.5px border in `#5D4037`. When selected: `#14532D` background with a crisp white check glyph.

### Lists & Telemetry Feeds
- **List Rows**: Divided by 1px subtle lines (`#E6DFD3`). Zebra striping utilizes alternating `#FFFFFF` and `#F8F7F0` (at 60% opacity).
- **Data Points**: Numbers rendered in tabular semi-bold text accompanied by micro-units styled in `#647067`.

### Bilingual Metric Indicator (Specialized Component)
- A dual-line operational element presenting key parameters (e.g., Moisture / ஈரப்பதம்). English title rendered in `label-sm` uppercase, Tamil subtitle set to `bilingual-hint`, and numeric readings displayed in `headline-lg` with dynamic status color coding.
